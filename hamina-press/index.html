The 2007 to 2011 window, reachable free

Off-domain corroboration and free routes

Yle is free to read and in several cases publishes in English. Two of the hardest Hamina stories are Yle's alone and appear nowhere in the Kymen Sanomat results. Group syndication also matters: wire stories filed under /uutissuomalainen/ carry an identical numeric ID across kouvolansanomat.fi, ess.fi, keski-uusimaa.fi and esaimaa.fi, and every case tested came back free. Locally desked /paikalliset/ stories get a different ID on each title, so those must be searched by headline.
