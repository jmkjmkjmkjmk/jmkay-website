What has been building underneath the delivery.

Running three markets to one standard took an apparatus: blank instruments, an evidence register, consent and citation rules, and a set of checks that refuse to ship work that cannot be defended. Most of it was built to survive the engagement rather than to serve it.

It is close to being something a second person could run.

Getting into a market and getting the interviews is largely packaged. Turning verified evidence into a finding, and a finding into something a client reads, is the part that is not written down yet — and that is the honest state of it rather than a sales position.

Worth an hour of conversation before Dallas, rather than a document.
