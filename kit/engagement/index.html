Engagement shape

What a market engagement is, phase by phase — what the client receives at each one, and what the client has to supply for it to work.

Six phasesAlways trueWhat sits insideRoute these

Six phases

In order, and the order matters — verification cannot start before the field work and findings cannot start before verification. Phases overlap; they do not swap.

01Scope and access

They receive

The market brief, the stakeholder universe, and the interview slate.

They supply

Site contacts, the claims you already publish, and one named person who can open doors.

02Field discovery

They receive

Long-form interviews in the market, on the record and consented. A first read within days of leaving the ground.

They supply

Nothing while we are there. That is the point of the phase.

03Verification

They receive

Every claim registered, sourced and given a verdict. The ones that do not hold are flagged with the reason they do not.

They supply

Access to your own source documents for anything you already publish.

04Findings

They receive

The assessment, the friction map, and the honest picture — including what did not hold.

They supply

One review pass from your team.

05Deliverables

They receive

Whatever the engagement bought: the report, the film, the champion profiles, the index score. Each one traceable to the register behind it.

They supply

Approvals, and any brand direction your team wants applied.

06Close-out and handover

They receive

The evidence base, the citation index and the market record, in a form your own team or your agency can work from.

They supply

A decision on what stays live.

True of every engagement

Four facts that hold whatever was bought, and that can be said to a client before anything is scoped.

What can sit inside it

The phases are the same whichever lines the engagement carries. What changes is what arrives at the deliverables phase.

1

Market Impact Assessment

A sourced evidence base: every claim registered and verdicted, the friction map, and the honest picture. Weeks rather than quarters.

2

Story & Champion Mapping

The story inventory and a consented champion map, plus the stakeholder universe — who the story touches, from officials to your own site staff.

3

Community Storytelling Program

Flagship film, champion profiles, short-form, photography and the activation toolkit — every asset traceable to the evidence base.

4

The Halo Index

A banded, annually re-scored measure of what a facility gives back, across five dimensions. Methodology published, negatives counted.

5

Community Intelligence

The registers, the stakeholder map and the champion record as a living system — institutional memory that survives turnover.

6

The Playbook & Certified Researchers

The codified method plus researchers trained and certified against it — the same standard everywhere, verified by gates rather than by hope.

7

The Program Portal

One always-current place: schedule, deliverables, assets with thumbnails, transcripts. Nobody has to ask.

8

Claim Validation

The claims you already publish, taken one at a time and verdicted against the record — the ones that hold with their sources attached, the ones that do not, flagged.
