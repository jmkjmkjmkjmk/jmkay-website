First call

Run a first conversation without needing anyone else on the line. Five questions, the list that turns the call, and the four conditions worth slowing down for.

Before you dial

Three things, and none of them takes long. Walking in knowing what they publish is the difference between a discovery call and a capability pitch.

01

Who is on the call, and what does each of them own.

02

What the company publishes today about that site — the impact page, the press release, the annual report line.

03

What the local press in that market has run this quarter.

Why now

Something moved, or they would not have taken the call. Tap the one that is true — it sets the clock and usually names the budget holder.

A permit, rezoning or public-comment fight.
An announcement, an expansion, or a second phase.
A reporter, a county board or a chief executive asking a question nobody can answer.
An agency that is not landing.
A score or a ranking they received and do not trust.
Nothing yet — they are early, and want to get it right.

The five questions

Ask them in order. Each one is short enough to ask and then stop talking, which is where the answer comes from.

What are you being asked to prove, and who is asking?

Listen for. A chief executive, a county board and a journalist are three different buyers with three different clocks. The answer sets the urgency and usually the budget holder.

What do you publish today about community impact, and where did those numbers come from?

Listen for. Listen for modelled figures with no local source. That is the claim-validation opening, and it is the fastest line to sell because their own footnote already concedes it.

If a sceptic wanted to check you, who would they call first?

Listen for. Most cannot name anyone. The inability to answer is the gap, and saying so plainly is more persuasive than any capability slide.

Has anyone spent time in the market listening, or is this all from the corporate side?

Listen for. “We ran a survey” is a no. A survey is not a record, and saying so gently costs nothing.

What is on the calendar in the next quarter that this has to be ready for?

Listen for. A real date means a real budget. No date means they are exploring, which is fine — it changes the follow-up, not the interest.

When to slow it down

Four conditions that make an engagement worse than no engagement. None of them is a reason to end the relationship — they are reasons to change what gets sold, or when.

They want a predetermined answer.

A validation nobody can fail is worthless to them and damaging to us.

They cannot give access to the market.

The method starts in the town. Without doors it is a desk exercise.

They want the score and the publishing in one contract.

Scoring a market absorbs that market's credible reviewers. It has to be separated.

Nothing is built or announced yet.

There is no record to measure. Stay in touch and revisit at announcement.

Before the file closes

Closing the file properly while the call is still fresh beats reconstructing it later.

Step 1

Log the answers while they are fresh.

Step 2

Name which of the eight lines the conversation was actually about.

Step 3

Route it for scoping — that is where a price and a date come from.

Step 4

Send the leave-behind if it has not gone already.

Step 5

Set the follow-up date before you close the file.

Clear the marks before the next call, or the last conversation's answers get read as this one's.
