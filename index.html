How I Work

From documentaries to corporate campaigns, the process is the same: define the destination, then build the story backward. The audience might be a boardroom or the general public — the method doesn't change.

José Baselga: Cancer's Fiercest Opponent
Executive Producer & Co-Writer — Feature Documentary
A feature documentary honoring the father of precision oncology. AstraZeneca needed a producer with the acumen to navigate pharma’s institutional machinery, the tenacity to coordinate production across three countries, and the instincts to hold a family’s grief, a corporation’s objectives, and a public controversy in a single honest narrative. 02
Ask a Scientist
Creator & Executive Producer — Video Series
Conceived and launched weeks into the pandemic, when no one in pharma was doing anything like it. I created the format, directed every early episode myself — one-on-one over Zoom with PhD scientists, coaching them to explain concepts like circulating tumor DNA to a six-year-old. No crew, no studio, just a laptop and the right questions. Became the most-watched content in AstraZeneca YouTube history. Shorty Award finalist.
