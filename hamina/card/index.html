Hamina packing · Aug 10–20 · carry-on + personal item · 9 nights

53–69°F daily swing  ·  rain most days  ·  ~10 mph Baltic wind  ·  16 hrs daylight, sun down ~21:15  ·  one wash ~day 4
The system

Base layer stays on all day, long sleeve always. Mid layer is the dial — sweater at 07:00, stowed by noon. Shell is weather-only and lives in the bag. Nothing drops below 51°F, so heavy gets no slots — that single cut is what makes carry-on work.

Wear on the plane 5

  • Waterproof hooded jacket — most-used item of the trip
  • Merino crewneck — doubles as the plane blanket
  • Long-sleeve base, the good one
  • Trouser #1 — wool-blend or tech, not the jeans
  • Waterproof shoes — heaviest pair on your feet

  • Fine-knit long-sleeve, dark×1
  • Fine-knit long-sleeve, warm tone×1
  • Button-down, brushed cotton / oxford×1
  • Button-down #2, chambray or check×1
  • Technical long-sleeve, quick-dry×1 worksite
  • Short-sleeve tee, dark×1
  • Short-sleeve, the presentable one×1

Mid layers 3

  • Merino sweater, mid-weight×1
  • Wool overshirt / chore coat×1 — the style lever; mid layer, outerwear and dinner jacket in one
  • Packable vest, thin down or fleece×1 — adds ~10°F, packs to nothing

Bottoms 3 · all dark

  • Trouser #1, wool-blend or tech×1
  • Trouser #2, dark twill×1 worksite: full length required
  • Dark jeans×1

No shorts — wrong for the weather, and banned on most worksites.

Socks & underlayer

  • Underwear, merino or synthetic×7
  • Merino socks×5 — non-negotiable
  • Everyday socks×2
  • Thin merino base-layer top×1
  • Sleep set×1

No cotton — it won't dry overnight and that breaks the wash plan.

Shoes 2 pairs, hard limit

  • Waterproof leather sneaker or low boot — worn; 8 of 10 days
  • Clean minimal leather sneaker
  • Confirm any worksite footwear rule — some sites require safety-toe, some issue it. The one thing you can't solve locally.

Swim & sauna

  • Swim shorts, quick-dry×2
  • Pool slides / flip-flops×1
  • Quick-dry towel×1 — most regional saunas are BYO
  • Wet bag / dry sack×1

Accessories

  • Light merino scarf — best warmth per gram; leave it off on any worksite
  • Brimmed cap
  • Sunglasses — low Nordic sun until 21:00
  • Packable tote or small daypack
  • Travel wash, small
  • Adapter, chargers, battery

Skip the umbrella — coastal wind wins. The hood is the plan.

Leave home

  • Anything heavy · the third pair of shoes · shorts · pale bottoms · umbrella · a suit that needs care · cotton socks and underwear · board shorts with a cotton liner · duplicate electronics

The wash · one load, ~day 4

Hotel laundry in a town this size runs next-day — a morning drop-off returns the following evening, an evening drop-off costs two days. Ask for the cut-off on arrival. With one wash mid-trip the back half is the longer stretch, which is why it's seven pairs not six. Sweaters and trousers never go in — air them overnight.

Backup: 24 Pesula at Prisma Kotka — the only self-service laundry in the region. Daily 07–22, ~35 min from Hamina, card payment, no booking.

The Fortress Trail · 4 km

Starts at the Flag Tower, runs the ramparts, ends at Town Hall Square. Walk it either direction, leave it anywhere. Seven boards with QR codes along the way:

1Flag Tower — the bulwarks
2Hämeenlinna Bastion — gunpowder
3Rauhankatu — wildlife in the embankments
4Central Bastion — the 58 vaults
5Vyborg gate — military town
6Reserve Officer School Park
7Town Hall Square

Pikkuympyräkatu is a full 1 km circle. Museum Street runs ~15 heritage sites in one street.
