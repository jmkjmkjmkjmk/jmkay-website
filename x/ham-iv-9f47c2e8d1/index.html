Sunday 16 August
Markku Porkka — Hallikulma Pub09:00 · FIRST
Filming: interiors + street b-roll while set up. Consent on camera before the interview proper.

Heat thread (lead)

  1. You were on the council when the heat-recovery project came through. What was the case made for it, in the room? Who pushed, who doubted?
  2. What did the town expect the project to do — and what has it actually done so far, as far as you can see?
  3. When people in Hamina talk about their heating today, what do they say? (never suggest bills got cheaper — if he raises prices, follow)
  4. What did the council need from Google or the utility to say yes? Anything promised that hasn't arrived?
  5. Who else was in those discussions? Minutes, a vote record, a presentation we could see?

The pub before/after

  1. What was this room like on a Friday night when the mill was running? And in the years after it closed?
  2. Who drinks here now that didn't fifteen years ago? What do you hear at the bar?
  3. The 6pm test: the town centre on an ordinary weekday evening — then and now.
  4. Has any of this reached you personally — the pub, your family — or does it sit at a distance from your own life?
  5. What would you be nervous to see in print about Hamina?

Apportionable general bank — any subject, any slot
The bankcarry always
  1. The anchor: "Since the campus came, what changed in your business, your street, your school, or your heating, that you did not see coming?"
  2. The counterfactual: "If Google wasn't here, what would that mean for you?"
  3. The personal-benefit probe: "Has any of this reached you personally, or does it sit at a distance from your own life?" — a "no" is a finding, not a failed interview.
  4. The wage-drain block (never yet asked in Hamina — put to any employer): Have you lost people to the campus or its contractors? To whom, for what work? · What's happened to what you have to pay to keep good people? · Is hiring easier or harder than five years ago? Why?
  5. The comparator (anyone over 50): "Towns like Kuusankoski lost their mills too. What happened there? Why the difference?"
  6. The 6pm test (any resident): "Describe the town centre at six on a weekday evening — now, and fifteen years ago."
  7. Documents-and-names close (every session): "Who else saw this happen? Is there a record — an invoice, minutes, photographs, a filing — behind what you've told us?"
  8. The landmine (every session): "Is there anything about Hamina you would be nervous to see in print?" — fallback: "What are we getting wrong?"
